Location
    • Tap a part to read what it does. Isolate it to study it alone, or hide it to see what's underneath.
    • Slice cuts the organ open. Choose front, side or top and slide the cut through it. Cut faces show the tissue inside.
    • Pull apart separates the parts so you can see how they fit together.
    • View in AR places the organ life-size on your floor or table. Drag with one finger to turn it, pinch to resize, and tap parts to study them.

    QR codes

    Each code opens the app straight to one organ. Paste your app's public link and the codes update, ready to screenshot or print.
